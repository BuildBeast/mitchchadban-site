### 2. Mumbrella — Nathan Jolly (njolly@mumbrella.com.au)

**Subject:** Opinion pitch: we're measuring AI visibility with the wrong word

Hi Nathan,

Richard Parker's "GEO paralysis" column argued that marketers shouldn't wait for a playbook. I agree, but there's a measurement problem underneath it that I'd like to argue in a 700-word opinion piece.

**The argument:** the industry uses "AI visibility" to mean three different things. Being *mentioned* in an answer, being *cited* as a source and *ranking* in the results underneath are separate outcomes. They respond to different work and need separate measurement. Brands reporting one number are optimising blind, and the tools selling "AI visibility scores" mostly blur the three.

**Structure:** one argument, Australian examples of how each outcome shows up, and what a marketing lead should ask their agency or tool vendor. No product, no services pitch, exclusive to Mumbrella.

I'm an Australian designer and marketer working on SEO and AEO. The longer version of the distinction is here: https://mitchchadban.com/the-difference-between-mentions-citations-and-rankings/
